### Operating History
As a part of the development of new products and markets, and our vision to deliver
integral solutions to our clients, ME Long Teng has developed technology, metallurgical as
well as process, for the manufacture of superior quality forged steel balls.
Through exclusive manufacturing agreements with Changshu Longteng Special Steel
Co., Ltd., ME Elecmetal’s new products “ME Super SAG” and “ME Ultra Grind” began to be
commercialized worldwide in 2009. In 2011, ME Elecmetal and Changshu Longteng Special
Steel Co., Ltd. incorporated, in China, to create the 50/50 joint-venture company, “ME Long
Teng Grinding Media (Changshu) Co. Ltd.” (“ME Long Teng”), for the manufacturing of these
products sold by ME Elecmetal worldwide. Subsequent to the launching of a pilot plant in
2010, ME Long Teng initiated in 2011 the construction of a modern manufacturing plant
for grinding balls, in the city of Changshu, China, with a total capacity of production of
about 500,000 Metric Tons of grinding balls, which ME Elecmetal directly commercializes
worldwide. In 2018, the fifth phase of this project was finalized.
### Grinding Media Production Capacity
Currently, the Joint Venture facility in China has an annual production capacity over
500,000 MT of forged steel balls, and 60,000 MT of grinding rods. The annual production
capacity of the facility in Zambia is over 100,000 MT of forged steel balls.
In addition to our facility in China and Zambia, ME Long Teng continues to evaluate options
for establishing more manufacturing facilities in other parts of the world, in line with our
business growth.

# Quality Assurance
# Procedures
It is critical for ME Long Teng to maintain a high level of quality and consistency in its products. The following
steps are fully executed and documented throughout the manufacturing process.
### A. ### Metallurgical Design
Based on specific application requirements at customer’s site, ME Long Teng designs the metallurgical
properties of its grinding media accordingly to ensure high level of compatibility between the grinding
ball product and the application requirement at customers’ site. Overall low wear rate is the end goal.
Such material designs must always be kept confidential.
### B. ### Raw Material
By using “clean steel” technology, ME Long Teng grinding balls have ultra-low impurity levels in the
alloy raw material, which ensures best final product quality. ME Long Teng specifications have the
lowest detrimental chemistry levels in the industry. To ensure the continuity of such competitive
advantage, the following quality control procedures must be followed with regards to raw material
management:
• The steel material designed by ME Long Teng is only sourced from the Long Teng steel mill that
has state of the art steel making equipment and processes. Quality audits are carried out regularly.
Use or purchase of steel material outside of the prequalified steel mills must be pre-approved by
the President of ME Long Teng in charge of technical and engineering function.
• All steel material must come with a quality certificate for each heat lot indicating conformity to
the specifications.
• For ME Super SAG balls material, S and P levels shall be maintained below 0.022%.
• For ME Ultra Grind balls or ME Performa material, S and P levels shall be maintained below 0.025%.
• All steel materials, upon receipt at the ball plant, shall be randomly sampled at 1-5% (depending
on the actual need) for compliance in chemistry, segregation, and metallurgical requirement to
ensure quality consistency at the ball plant lab.

# Quality Assurance
# Procedures
### C. ### Production Batch management
Based on actual production need, customer order, and plant loading, ball production for a ME Super
SAG batch can be established in the range of 100 MT to 1000 MT each batch. Each batch is assigned to
one batch serial number, which shall be linked to the following:
1. Bar heat number (traceability back to raw material)
2. Quantity of each bar heat
3. Heat treatment records for the ball batch production
4. All recordable instrumentation reading during the production
Since ME Ultra Grind ball production is a continuous process (different from ME Super SAG ball’s
batch process), Ultra Grind production and material records shall be monitored and maintained on a
continuous time-based process basis without being assigned a production batch serial number.
### D. ### Heat treatment
The proprietary heat treatment processes and procedures at ME Long Teng must always be kept
confidential. Variance at each heat treatment stage must be controlled within plus or minus 20 degrees
Celsius of the designed temperature.
All heat treatment process data shall be regularly collected, maintained, and sent to ME Long Teng for
statistics production control analysis and no products with heat treatment cracks are allowed to be
delivered or shipped.
### E. ### Forging
Due to the ME Long Teng high automated forging processes, the roundness of the grinding media is
excellent, with very small tolerance of diameter or weight differences. Both the rolling line and forging
line are done at high temperature

# Quality Assurance
# Procedures
### F. ### Physical and Chemistry Tests
Following physical tests shall be conducted on 1% samples randomly collected from each
production batch, before products are qualified to be shipped.
1. Hardness section and profiling (1 to 20 balls from each batch, depending on the batch size,
lab work schedule, product maturity, and customer requirement). Hardness acceptance
criteria are based on product specification.
2. Lab chemistry inspection if required by customers (1 to 20 balls from each batch, depending
on the batch size, lab work schedule, product maturity, and customer requirement).
Chemistry acceptance criteria are based on product specification.
3. Drop ball testing (1 to 20 balls from each batch, depending on the batch size lab work
schedule, product development stage, and customer requirement) Ball sample passing drop
test simulating one-hour SAG mill grind out without half ball breakage is considered
acceptable.
a. Ball sample showing half ball breakage in drop test simulating one-hour SAG
mill grind out is considered not acceptable. If breakage is found at ball drop
test, ball sampling size is required to be tripled, and then quadrupled. If over
half of the balls sampled show breakage in the drop test, the whole batch
shallbe rejected.
b. Ball spalling during drop test simulating one-hour SAG mill grind out without
half ball breakage is considered acceptable. However, excessive spalling
during test needs to be reported, as it is an indicated of potential need for ball
design adjustment.
### G. ### Visual & Surface Inspection
All following visual tests shall be conducted on samples randomly collected at 5% of the quantity of
each production batch, before products are shipped.
ü Ball weight (nominal with plus or minus 10% allowed)
ü Surface quality (balls samples with obvious forging marks, forging laps, or forging belts shall be
within 10% of the balls sampled)
ü Instruction from specific customer, if any, shall be followed.
### H. ### Package & Shipment
The production serial number for the product shall be written on the top of each bulk bag to ensure
full traceability back to plant records.
Delivery will be made according to the customers requirements.
