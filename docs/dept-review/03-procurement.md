## B. Current content on the site

### Our Approach (3 paragraphs)
- Procurement is an important part of reliable, efficient, sustainable operations.
- Focus on quality goods/services while promoting responsibility, transparency, integrity, value for money.
- Build professional, mutually beneficial relationships with suppliers who share our commitments.

### What We Procure (10 categories)
Industrial Supplies & Equipment · Engineering & Maintenance Services · Raw
Materials & Consumables · PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) · Transport &
Logistics · ICT · Professional & Technical Services · Facilities & General
Services · Environmental & Safety Services · Other Operational Requirements

### Procurement Principles (6)
1. **Integrity** — honesty, professionalism, ethical conduct
2. **Quality** — meet required technical/operational/quality standards
3. **Safety** — HSE considerations in supplier relationships
4. **Fairness & Transparency**
5. **Value for Money** — quality, reliability, technical suitability, service capability, commercial value
6. **Local Participation** — support qualified Zambian businesses/local supply chains

### Working With Suppliers
- Value reliability, technical capability, competitive offerings, responsible practices.
- Suppliers may be asked to demonstrate qualifications, licences, certifications, experience, capacity, compliance.

### Responsible Procurement
- Business partners must comply with applicable laws, business ethics, health & safety, environmental responsibility, professional conduct.

### Future Procurement Notices
- Says opportunities "will be communicated through official ME Long Teng channels" and suppliers should **monitor this website** for future announcements. *(No notice mechanism exists on the site yet.)*
